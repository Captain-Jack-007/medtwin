# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People in Navoiy communities completing a guided smartphone screening, and care teams reviewing screening evidence. Users may be under stress, on constrained devices, or working with intermittent connectivity, so the primary task must remain clear and resilient.

## Product Purpose

MedTwin provides privacy-conscious wellness and clinical decision-support screening from real user reports and available device sensors. It must communicate measurement provenance, distinguish missing data from normal findings, and support professional review without presenting itself as a diagnostic device.

## Brand Personality

Calm, credible, precise. The interface should feel technically capable without becoming theatrical, and clinically cautious without feeling cold or obstructive.

## Anti-references

Avoid fictional medical telemetry, decorative hospital dashboards, diagnosis-like certainty, generic wellness-app optimism, and sensor experiences that animate independently of real input.

## Design Principles

- Show evidence, source, and quality together.
- Prefer an honest unavailable state to an invented result.
- Keep the guided task obvious under pressure.
- Use motion only to reveal live device state.
- Separate real personal scans from regional demo data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, explicit permission states, readable error recovery, and layouts that remain usable on phones and desktop browsers.
